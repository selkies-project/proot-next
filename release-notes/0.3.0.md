# proot-next 0.3.0

## Nested proot and bwrap

A proot or bwrap started inside the guest now works. It cannot be a second
supervisor (the kernel allows one tracer per process and one seccomp
listener per filter chain), so it is not one: an `execve` of proot-next's
own binary, or of any program named `proot`, `proot-next` or `bwrap`, is
served by the supervisor already there. The command line is parsed on the
caller's behalf, every path in it is composed through the caller's view,
and the process that called `proot` becomes the command, in a view of its
own that its children inherit. The caller's child, exit status and signals
are the kernel's own, and nesting composes to any depth.

- proot-apps' graphical installer runs `proot -R app /install` from inside
  its own guest again.
- GTK's glycin image loaders run inside their bwrap sandbox root instead of
  unsandboxed, through the images' `bwrap` symlink or the rootfs's own
  bubblewrap (FileZilla under proot-apps).
- bwrap's `--info-fd`, `--json-status-fd`, `--seccomp`, `--args`,
  `--file`, `--die-with-parent`, `--new-session`, `--clearenv` and
  `--setenv` behave as at top level; `--version`, `--help` and usage errors
  are reported on the caller's own stdout and stderr.
- A nested `-0` or `-k` needs the outer launch to have the same option,
  since the seccomp filter is fixed at start-up, and is refused with a
  message otherwise. `PROOT_NEST=0` turns off the match by name.

The exec trampoline, which carried out every exec without ptrace, now
carries out nested launches in both modes; its record format changed to
carry the environment, the working directory, the caller's seccomp programs
and the status descriptors.

## Fixes

- The exec trampoline builds the argument and environment vectors it hands
  to `execve` in zeroed storage written through its own address, rather
  than in arrays seeded with null pointers.
